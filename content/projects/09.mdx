An AI career-intelligence platform built at Vibe ATL @ Georgia Tech in under 12 hours. It deep-parses resumes and transcripts into a multi-dimensional competency profile — Technical, Domain, Soft Skills, and Academic Rigor — via LLMs, then cross-references against job descriptions.

Features include AI skill extraction (Gemini + OpenAI), a dynamic 6-axis radar graph (Chart.js), a Chrome extension for real-time fit scoring over LinkedIn, Indeed, and Handshake, and a progressive toolkit of cover letters and resume edits unlocked at a fit score of 65 or higher.
